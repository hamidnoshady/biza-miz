## Noticed — reported, not fixed (AGENTS.md rule 6)

- **[Fixed in F1]** **Stale `item_stock.total_value_rial` (all retail trades, pre-existing on main).** Non-batch writers change `item_stock.quantity` without the carrying value: sales (`accessories-service`, `trade-goods-service`, `cosmetics-service`, `retail-online-sale-service`), `shipItemTransfer`, supplier returns, void/reversal restocks and the new customer-return restock (which mirrors the void pattern). The warehouse **issue** document values non-batch relief as `total_value_rial × qty / quantity`, so after any sale or transfer it over-relieves. Repro: receive 10 @ 50,000 → sell 4 → issue 1 damaged → relieved **83,333** (not 50,000); 5 on hand worth 250,000 but GL 1374 = 216,667. Needs one owner-approved fix across the item-stock writers.
- **[Fixed in F2]** **Product-form opening quantity writes stock with no journal entry.** `createProductRecord` → `writeOpeningValues` → `receiveStock` puts quantity on `item_stock` without posting, so inventory exists off the books; conflicts with the epic's "product creation alone does not silently post stock". Shared across retail trades and UI-visible — needs a product decision (post an opening-balance entry, or route through a receipt).
- **Sell-through by collection uses current tags.** `merchandising-service.sellThroughByCollection` joins live `items.collection/season`, so re-tagging moves past sales between collections in that report (posted facts are untouched). Phase-5 item.

Shared report SQL for every trade-goods trade.
